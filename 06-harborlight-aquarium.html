Meet the exhibit

A forest that grows two feet a day

Giant kelp is one of the fastest-growing organisms on Earth. Our tank is fed by fresh seawater pumped straight from the bay, so the forest you see is alive with the same plankton, fish and invertebrates you'd find a mile offshore.

Curator Dr. Ana Ruiz sat down with us to talk about how the exhibit was built — and what it took to grow the forest from seedlings.

Rescued & rehabilitated

Juniper, Pike & Moss

All three of our sea otters were stranded as pups and couldn't be returned to the wild. Today they help researchers learn how otters keep kelp forests healthy — by eating the sea urchins that would otherwise mow them down.

In the video, keep an eye on Juniper — she has a trick at dinnertime that no one taught her.

The Jelly Gallery at dawn
