Introduction to Woodworking

Learn safe use of hand and power tools while building a small cherry side table you'll take home.

Instructor
Hal Brennerman
Location
Trades Center, Shop 114
Length
6 weeks, 12 hours
Fee
$289 + $65 materials

Shop rules: closed-toe shoes and safety glasses are required at every session. Long hair must be tied back.

Refund & withdrawal policy

Withdraw at least 7 days before the first class meeting for a full refund minus a $15 processing fee.

Withdrawals 1–6 days before the first class receive a 50% refund. No refunds are issued after the first class meeting.

If Cedar Valley cancels a course for low enrollment, you will receive a full refund including materials fees within 10 business days.

Need help registering? Call the Continuing Ed office at (555) 501-2200, Monday–Friday 8am–5pm, or stop by the Welcome Center in Building A.
