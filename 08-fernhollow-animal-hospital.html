New to Fernhollow?

We're glad you found us. New clients can book online any time, or call the front desk during business hours. Ask about our senior pet program and our multi-pet discount.

Please bring your pet's vaccination records to the first visit. If your previous clinic can email them, we'll add them to your file before you arrive.

To get started, use the green button in the top right corner of the page. To download our new client form, click here. You can also read our new client FAQ.
