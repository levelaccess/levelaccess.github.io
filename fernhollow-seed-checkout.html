    • For every packet you borrow, you’ll return at least one packet of saved seed by December 31 of that year.
    • Seed you return becomes the property of the Fernhollow Seed Library.
    • You release Fernhollow Seed Co. from claims about germination, crop yield, or cross-pollination.
