Set up recurring invoices

Updated 3 days ago·5 min read

Recurring invoices let you bill the same client on a regular schedule without recreating the invoice every time. Tallyhand creates a fresh draft from your template, applies your net terms, and sends it automatically on each billing period.

Available on
Growth and Business plans
Who can do this
Owners and Admins
Time to set up
About 5 minutes
Works with
Card, ACH, and manual payments

Before you begin

– Make sure the client already exists in Clients.
– Connect a payment method if you want automatic collection.
– Decide whether line items will stay the same every period.

Note: Changes to a schedule only affect future invoices. Invoices that have already been sent keep their original amounts, dates and terms, and you'll need to edit or void them individually.

Create a schedule

1. Click the gear icon in the upper-right corner of any page and choose Invoicing.

2. Open Recurring and click the round green button on the right.

3. Choose the client and an invoice template from the panel on the left.

4. Set the start date and how often to send: weekly, monthly, quarterly, or a custom interval.

5. Press Ctrl + S or click Save schedule.

Important: If a client's card fails, the invoice stays in Sent and we retry three times over seven days. After the third failure the schedule pauses until you update the payment method.

Common questions

Can I change the amount for just one period? Yes. Open the upcoming draft from the schedule and edit it before the send date. You can read more about editing drafts here.

What time do invoices send? At 9:00am in your workspace's time zone. You can change your time zone in workspace settings.
