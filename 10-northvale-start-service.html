Tip: Wash clothes in cold water. About 90% of a washer's energy goes to heating water.

Tip: Unplug chargers and small appliances when you're not using them.

Stopping service?

Give us at least two business days' notice. We'll take a final meter reading on your stop date, and your final bill will be mailed to your forwarding address within 10 days. Any deposit is applied to that bill first.
