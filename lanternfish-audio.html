Why does bread rise?

Priya and Ben put two bowls of dough side by side. Kitchen Table Science, video edition. 27 seconds. Captions on.

Read the transcript

Priya: So this is a ball of dough I mixed about two hours ago. And this one is the same recipe, mixed five minutes ago.

Ben: The difference is yeast. Yeast eats the sugars in the flour and gives off carbon dioxide and a little bit of alcohol.

Priya: The gluten network traps those gas bubbles, like tiny balloons.

Ben: Warmer kitchen, faster rise. Too warm, though, and the yeast dies off.
